About

Internet is a small network made of three parts: a registry that maps names to addresses, nodes that serve files, and clients that browse them.

The three parts

PartWhat it doesCommand
RegistryMaps names to addressesinternet registry
NodeServes a folder under a nameinternet serve
ClientFetches internet:// pagesinternet get

Publish your own site

  2. Press Publish to host it
  3. Share the internet:// link
